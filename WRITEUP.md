# Challenge Description

## Prompt given to players:
"Mord has escaped! 🕳️📷
Zookeepers need your help before he digs too deep into the city’s tunnels. He’s leaving digital tracks—clues hidden in posts, stories & tags. Use your OSINT skills to track him down before he wakes up!"

Before starting the challenge, players are given this prompt as a guide to begin the puzzle. By reading through the prompt there are subtle hints helping players. No one but our group really knows who Mord really is but "before he digs too deep into the city's tunnels" is a hint to Mord being a mole. "Digital tracks-clues hidden in posts, stories & tags" this line is a clue hinting to where players should begin their investigation which is Instagram. The last line "before he wakes up!" is the last clue hinting to what the flag starts off with before finding the rest of the flag.

## Step 1: The Zoo’s Instagram – @zooscapesdream

The starting point was the official zoo account. Browsing through posts, players found one photo showing Mord munching on his favourite food.

Hidden in the background was subtle branding: a takeaway cup/shopping bag with the @BerryGoodCafe logo.

Technique: Careful image inspection, spotting hidden details in the background.
Next Step: Follow the café’s Instagram profile.

## Step 2: Café Account – @BerryGoodCafe

On this page, the latest post featured a new seasonal drink. Nothing in the caption was useful, but reading the comments revealed a regular customer account: @brewsandwalk.

Technique: Comment inspection and social graph pivoting.
Next Step: Investigate @brewsandwalk.

## Step 3: The regular customer - @brewandwalks → @walkswithluna

Players will find a picture of Mord in their story highlight which indicates that the players are on the right track.
At first glance, @brewsandwalk mostly posted coffee shots and city strolls. But digging deeper, players noticed them following and tagging a friend account and well as following them: @walkswithluna.

Technique: Looking at tags/friends to trace social connections.
Next Step: Visit @walkswithluna.

## Step 4: The Friend – @walkswithluna → @tashiebee → @urbanxplorer1

On @walkswithluna, players discovered a comment exchange with @tashiebee.
In this thread, @tashiebee mentioned checking out @urbanxplorer1 — the next breadcrumb.

Technique: Comment analysis and social graph following.
Next Step: Explore @urbanxplorer1.

## Step 5: The Explorer – @urbanxplorer1 → @citydeptmaintenance

@urbanxplorer1 mainly posted urban exploration content. Checking their follow list revealed they were following @citydeptmaintenance, an unusual link.

On this account, one post referenced “Mord’s burrow”. Beneath it, a comment from @dailycommute92 pointed to the next step.

Technique: Inspecting follower/following connections and scanning comments.
Next Step: Go to @dailycommute92.

## Step 6: The Commuter – @dailycommute92

This account contained photos of tunnels, underground passages, and ruins. The real trail, however, was hidden in the story highlights.

By going through the highlights, players eventually uncovered an image with faint embedded text revealing the flag: HM2025(MORD_IS_SLEEPING_AT_`LOCATION_NAME_STORAGE_N0`)
